## Reflection & Online Auto-Discovery
- **Reflect on Match Authenticity**: Evaluate whether any candidate in the database is *truly* the exact, most relevant problem available online for what was taught.

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- "Majority Element" (Boyer-Moore) vs "Majority Element II" (modified Boyer-Moore for >n/3)
- "Single Number" (XOR all) vs "Single Number II" (bit counting) vs "Single Number III" (XOR + grouping)
- "Subarray Sum Equals K" (prefix sum + hashmap) vs "Maximum Subarray" (Kadane's)
- "Two Sum" (hashmap) vs "Two Sum II" (two pointers, sorted array)
